A Note from Sarah

Homes are defined by the people who live within them.

I believe the most memorable homes are not defined by a particular trend or style. They are defined by the people who live within them.

A thoughtfully designed home should feel collected rather than manufactured, polished without being overly formal, and beautiful while still supporting everyday life. It should reflect your experiences, your surroundings, and the way you want to live.

I founded Sevin Design to provide clients with a more personal and attentive design experience; one built upon trust, thoughtful communication, and an appreciation for the details that give a home lasting character.

My role is not simply to select beautiful furnishings and finishes. It is to listen carefully, recognize possibilities, anticipate challenges, and help bring clarity to the many decisions involved in creating a home. Whether we are designing a single room, renovating an existing residence, or collaborating on a new construction project, my goal is to make the experience feel considered, organized, and deeply personal.

It is a privilege to be invited into a client’s home and entrusted with its transformation. I approach every project with gratitude, discretion, and a genuine commitment to creating a home that feels entirely your own.

Sarah Sevin

Meet Sarah

Founder & Principal Designer

Sarah Sevin is the founder and principal designer of Sevin Design, a Northern Michigan interior design studio specializing in thoughtful, timeless residential interiors.

Educated at Harrington College of Design in Chicago, Sarah brings a practiced eye for proportion, material, color, and composition to every project. Her approach combines refined design with an understanding of how homes must function in everyday life.

Based in Harbor Springs, Sevin Design serves clients throughout Northern Michigan and beyond.
